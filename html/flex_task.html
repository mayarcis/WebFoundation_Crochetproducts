BMW

BMW is a German multinational company which currently produces
luxury automobiles and motorcycles
also produced aircraft engines until 1945. The company was founded
in 1916 as a manufacturer of aircraft engines
which it produced from 1917 until 1918 and again from 1933 to 1945.

Mercedes

Mercedes-Benz is a German automotive company known for producing
luxury cars, buses, and commercial vehicles.
The Mercedes-Benz brand was established in 1926 through the merger
of Benz & Cie. and Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft.
The company is known for its luxury vehicles, advanced technology,
and high-quality engineering.

Ford

Ford is an American multinational automotive company.
It was founded by Henry Ford in 1903 and is headquartered in Dearborn, Michigan.
The company produces cars, trucks, and commercial vehicles.
